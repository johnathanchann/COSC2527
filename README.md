## Frog game 
You've done bad things in your past life and now karma get's you for the best. 
You've now been reincarnated as a frog and you must live like a frog. You must eat the local delight (flies)
to stay alive.
There are a few snakes that sees you as food wandering around the jungle and you must avoid them.

## Manual Controls
- **Left click**: Switch between human and AI  
- **Press 0**: Decision Tree v1  
- **Press 1**: Decision Tree v2  
- **Press 2**: Behavior Tree
  
---
## Game Objective
- Eat 10 flies to win the game
- Avoid the snake
